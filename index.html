About

Research built around the business question—not just the dataset.

I combine a Bachelor of Computer Engineering with an MBA in Marketing, allowing me to evaluate technology markets from both product and commercial perspectives. My research experience spans end-to-end market studies, competitive benchmarking, vendor profiling, qualitative and quantitative synthesis, client engagement and thought-leadership delivery.
